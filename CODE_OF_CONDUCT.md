# Code of Conduct

Contributors and participants are expected to communicate respectfully and help others evaluate ideas on their merits.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differences in experience and perspective.
- Protect private information and avoid posting credentials or confidential material.
- Describe problems and evidence without attacking the people involved.
- Accept reasonable moderation intended to keep discussions productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, impersonation, and repeated disruption are not welcome.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. If a private reporting channel is not available, ask for one without publishing personal or sensitive details.

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed discussion spaces.
